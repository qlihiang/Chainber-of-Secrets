Returns & Exchanges

Exchanges within 5 days.

Every piece is handmade, so here is how we make things right.

Each piece is handmade in small numbers, and many are one of a kind, so we can't offer change-of-mind refunds. We do offer exchanges and store credit, and we will always make things right if something arrives damaged.

Exchanges and store credit

  • Email us within 5 days of receiving your order.
  • The piece must be unworn and undamaged, and returned in its original packaging.
  • You can exchange it for another piece of the same value (paying any difference), or receive store credit for the full amount.
  • Store credit is valid for 6 months and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Return postage is paid by you. We recommend tracked mail, as we can't be responsible for returns lost in the post.
  • Each order can be exchanged once.

Not eligible for exchange

  • Pieces that have been worn, altered, resized or damaged after delivery.
  • Custom or personalised orders.
  • Items bought on sale, unless they are faulty.

Damaged or faulty pieces

If your piece arrives damaged, or something isn't right with how it was made, email us within 3 days of receiving it with photos. We'll repair it, replace it, or give you a full refund, and we'll cover the postage.

Our workmanship promise

Chainmaille is woven ring by ring, and with everyday wear a ring can occasionally loosen. For 30 days after delivery, we'll repair any workmanship issue for free. After that, we're still happy to repair your piece at a small cost. Just get in touch.

Normal wear and tear, and damage from accidents or from not following our jewellery care tips, aren't covered.

How to start a return or exchange

  1. Email chainberofsecrets@gmail.com with your name, order details and the reason for your return. For damaged items, include photos.
  2. We'll reply with the return address and next steps.
  3. Once we receive and check the piece, we'll send your exchange or store credit within 5 working days.
